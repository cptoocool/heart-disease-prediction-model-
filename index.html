About the Clinical Features

• ST Slope: The slope of the ST segment during peak exercise. An upsloping pattern is typical in healthy hearts, whereas flat or downsloping patterns strongly correlate with myocardial ischemia.
• Oldpeak (ST Depression): The difference in ST segment level between resting state and peak exercise. Higher values indicate compromised coronary perfusion.
• Exercise Angina: Transient retrosternal discomfort induced by cardiac workload due to supply/demand mismatch.
• Cholesterol & Fasting BS: Systemic metabolic biomarkers contributing to atherosclerotic plaque accumulation.
